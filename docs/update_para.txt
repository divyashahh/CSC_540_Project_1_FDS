We made a few changes to improve the original ER model and better match our final database. We clarified both ISA relationships as total and disjoint and clearly showed the separate origin and destination relationships between DEPARTURE and HUB. These updates support a normalized design in BCNF by keeping attributes dependent on the appropriate keys and avoiding unnecessary redundancy. We also removed the HUB address attribute because it was not required for the final application or queries, while the hub’s region provides the geographic grouping needed by the system.
